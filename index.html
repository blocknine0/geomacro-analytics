How the pipeline is actually running

Geomacro runs as a fully autonomous pipeline: news comes in, an AI model scores how severe it is, a market gets created, and the result settles onchain, all without a human touching any step. Everything below is pulled live from the production database plus read-only Arc Testnet verification against both the legacy V1 contract V1 and the V2 proxy V2. Nothing here is sampled, mocked, or estimated. Unavailable data remains unavailable.
